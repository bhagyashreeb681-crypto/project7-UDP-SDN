PROJECT 7: UDP PACKET LOSS DETECTION AND RECOVERY

EXPERIMENT 1: NORMAL NETWORK

Total Packets: 20
Transmission Attempts: 20
Retries: 0
Successful Packets: 20
Failed Packets: 0
Acknowledgment Percentage: 100%
Execution Time: 4.18 seconds


EXPERIMENT 2: SIMULATED 30% PACKET LOSS

Total Packets: 20
Transmission Attempts: 31
Retries: 11
Successful Packets: 18
Failed Packets: 2
Recovery Percentage: 90%
Execution Time: 30.15 seconds


OBSERVATION:

In the normal network, all 20 packets were successfully acknowledged without retransmission.

During simulated packet loss, the client performed 11 additional transmission attempts.

18 packets were successfully acknowledged, while 2 packets remained unsuccessful after the maximum retry attempts.

The experiment demonstrates the use of timeout detection and retransmission to improve UDP packet delivery reliability.
